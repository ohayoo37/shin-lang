# Community code of conduct

SHIN welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. Be respectful, give specific and constructive feedback, and criticize ideas rather than people. Make room for questions and acknowledge others' work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, spam and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable in project spaces. Do not retaliate against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock a thread, warn participants or restrict participation according to the circumstances. Where practical, they will explain the decision without disclosing sensitive information. Maintainers are expected to follow the same standards.

For public conduct concerns without sensitive details, ask the maintainer @ohayoo37 for moderation in the relevant thread. Do not post personal information or evidence that would expose someone. There is currently no dedicated confidential conduct mailbox; for abuse requiring confidential handling, use GitHub's built-in Report content or Report abuse flow. Do not use the security-vulnerability channel for general conduct reports.

日本語：経験・言語・背景を問わず歓迎します。人ではなく内容を議論し、誹謗中傷・差別・脅迫・個人情報の公開をしないでください。公開で扱える問題は保守担当者に相談し、機微な情報を伴う被害はGitHubの通報機能を利用してください。
